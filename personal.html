About

My name is Daming Yang, or in Chinese, "羊达明". My surname in Chinese means Aries. Currently, I am working on an Astronomy master program in Leiden University in the Netherlands. My master project on high-z quasar at Leiden is advised by Prof. Joseph Hennawi, Dr. Riccardo Nanni and Dr. Jan-Torge Schindler. My first master project on TDE at Leiden was advised by Prof. Sjoert van Velzen (Yes, this program at Leiden basically requires two theses). Prior to Leiden, I was a bachelor student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in Hefei, China. I was enrolled into the School of the Gifted Young (SGY) in USTC, and later followed the track of Astronomy in my senior year. My undergraduate thesis advisor was Prof. Jun-Xian Wang, and my thesis was on the study of the AGN duty cycle. I am a fan of Nintendo, especially the Zelda series and Splatoon. Cheers!
