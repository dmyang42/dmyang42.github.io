Research Projects

2023–Now

Euclid High-z Quasar

We discovered 31 quasars at redshifts 6.6–7.8 with Euclid, including 12 at z > 7. Two of them, at z = 7.77 and 7.69, are the most distant quasars ever observed.

2022–2023

High-z Quasar Candidate Archive

A spectroscopic study of 176 high-redshift quasar candidates observed with Keck, bringing their classifications into a single archive and using the sample to improve candidate selection and follow-up strategies for future surveys.

2021–2022

TDE Host Galaxies

An investigation of whether tidal disruption event spectral types are connected to the stellar populations and broader properties of their host galaxies, combining multi-wavelength photometry with statistical analysis.
